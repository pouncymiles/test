    How to use

    • Navigate: drag with two fingers or the Pan tool, pinch or scroll to zoom. Right/middle mouse drag also pans.
    • Territories → Fill: pick a nation, tap land. The fill spreads until it meets the coast, an orange landmark, or a drawn line. Choose Unclaimed to erase.
    • Landmark: tap a river or mountain range to toggle it as a border. Checkboxes make every river/range a border.
    • Freehand / Lines: draw your own boundary. Lines: tap points, double-tap (or Finish) to end, tap the first point to close a loop.
    • Brush: paint territory directly, ignoring borders.
    • Resources: a separate layer you can hide. Pen draws freehand; Shape places an icon; Eraser removes what you tap.
    • Install: on iPhone/iPad use Share → Add to Home Screen. In Chrome/Edge on Mac or Windows use the install icon in the address bar.
    • Shortcuts: Ctrl/⌘+Z undo, Shift for redo, Esc cancel, Enter finish line, +/- zoom, hold Space to pan.
